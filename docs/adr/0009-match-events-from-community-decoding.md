# Match Events: keep EA's unnamed counters whole, name them from community research

EA sends four fields for every player in a Match, `match_event_aggregate_0` to `_3`: comma-separated `code:count` pairs such as `217:5,24:12`. EA documents none of the codes. Until November 2026 we left them in the Raw Payload (ADR 0003), unparsed. They turn out to hold most of what a stats site wants and EA's named fields lack: shots on target, yellow cards, fouls, interceptions, dribbles, passes by direction and length, where the ball is won and lost, and EA's own feedback on positioning and pass choices.

Decisions (CLUBS-84):

- **Store the events whole.** Each Appearance keeps every pair EA sent as one JSON map of code to count (`appearances.events`), not a column per stat. A code decoded later, or a reading that changes, then needs a code change only: no migration and no re-reading of Raw Payloads. Stats are totalled in the stats layer from loaded rows, as the named stats already are, so nothing needs the counts in SQL.
- **Names come from community research, confirmed tier only.** The catalogue in `app/ea/events.ts` follows https://github.com/Interactive-63/eafc-pro-clubs-api-research, which checked its readings against about 51,000 player rows of FC 27. Our own first attempt at decoding, on 655 rows, got the exact identities right (goals, assists, shots on and off target, passes) and several others wrong, which is why we follow the larger study. Its "partial" and "exploratory" codes get no name and never reach a page.
- **EA's named stats stay the source for what they name.** Goals, assists, shots, passes, tackles, red cards and saves still come from the named fields. On our data event 214 equals goals and 11 equals assists exactly, and 217 + 218 equals shots in all but a stray row, but completed passes (215) are often one to four below `passesmade`, and tackles differ more. So a page never mixes the two sources inside one figure: "pass completion" uses the named fields, "forward passes" the events.
- **Old Matches are backfilled by a migration** from the Raw Payload each Match was first parsed from, which is what ADR 0003 kept them for.

Consequences: the readings are only as good as the research and can break with a new FC release, since the codes are EA's internals. If a code changes meaning, correct it in `app/ea/events.ts`; the stored counts are untouched. Friendlies and Opponents get events too, so an Opponent's players can serve as a yardstick for ours. A player who quits keeps their events even though EA zeroes their named stats, which CLUBS-87 uses to restore them.
